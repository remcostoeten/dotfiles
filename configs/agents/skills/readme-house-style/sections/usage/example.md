```markdown
## Usage

Search your home and office address once. The route is drawn on the map and its
distance becomes the round trip, which you can still overwrite, along with the rate
per kilometre.

Every office day is a click on the calendar. If you go in on fixed weekdays, fill the
month from those in one action and remove the exceptions. A day takes an optional
note.

When a month is done, close it. A closed month keeps the distance and rate it was
closed with, so a later rate change never rewrites old reimbursements, and refuses
new days until you reopen it.

Preview the sheet, then download it or email it to HR from the app. Each export is
logged with the months, days, kilometres and amount it contained.
```
