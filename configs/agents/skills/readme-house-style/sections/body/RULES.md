# Body

One or two short paragraphs, then bullets. The paragraph earns its place by saying
the one thing the bullets cannot: what the product actually is, or the assumption it
rejects. Papertrail's opens with "Most resume editors get this backwards"; skriuw's
opens with "Your workspace is a SQLite database on your machine". Both then point at
a live URL to try. If there is no live URL, point at the latest release, or drop the
pointer.

Bullets are one line each, seven at most (five for a library), describing behaviour
a user would notice. Bold at most one phrase per bullet, and only where a reader
skimming needs to land.

Close the section with a single line pointing at the deeper docs, linking two or
three files by name rather than summarising them. Only link files that exist:
`docs/FEATURES.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/deployment.md` in the template are
the usual names, not a requirement. If the repo has none, drop the closing line.
