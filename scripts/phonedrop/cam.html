Camera needs a secure connection

iOS only lets a page use the camera over HTTPS. Trust phonedrop's local certificate once, then the camera page opens over HTTPS. The certificate is limited to phonedrop.local and LAN addresses.

  1. Tap Get certificate and allow the profile download. If nothing happens, open this page in Safari first.
  2. Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → phonedrop local CA → Install.
  3. Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → turn on phonedrop local CA.
  4. Tap Open camera. You can add that page to your home screen too.
